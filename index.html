desk

desk は、開発者本人が自分の Gmail の受信を読み、返事に判断が要るものだけを本人の Discord に知らせるための、個人用の道具です。一般には提供していません。

desk is a personal tool used only by its developer. It reads the developer's own Gmail inbox and notifies the developer, on the developer's own Discord, about messages that need a decision. It is not offered to anyone else.
